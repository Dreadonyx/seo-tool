About Acme Widgets

Acme Widgets was founded in 2019 in Chennai by Priya Raman and Arun Kumar. The company maintains open-source widget testing tools that more than 1,200 engineering teams use in continuous integration pipelines.

Our mission is to make widget testing reliable, fast and free. Every tool we publish is released under the MIT license, documented in plain language, and tested against real-world widget suites before release.

Our team

The team includes eight engineers and two technical writers who work remotely across India and Europe. You can reach us through the contact page or on GitHub.
